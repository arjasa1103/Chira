## Problem Statement

The team wants Chira to make money, not only measure calibration. Four candidate
directions were brought in:

- **Profit 1:** add player analysis, rate teams, bet every game on the highest-rated team.
- **Profit 2:** compare model predictions with available odds and bet the difference.
- **Data 1:** add odds from other betting platforms.
- **Data 2:** research whether player/team stats affect the odds.

The work must run **in parallel** with the semester deliverable (weeks 7-11, hard deadline
end of November 2026) without contaminating its sealed holdout (the 2025-26 season,
PREREGISTRATION.md:258). Venue: **both Polymarket and sportsbooks, cross-venue.**

## Verdict on the four ideas

| Idea | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Profit 1 | **Dropped as written** | The price already reflects team strength; betting the favorite every game pays the fee on every bet for no edge. Historical player-availability timing does not exist (nba_api inactives arrive post-game), so a player edge cannot be backtested. |
| Profit 2 | **Kept, but not with the Chira model as the driver** | The pre-registered expectation is that the model trails the market by 0.02-0.03 Brier. Betting a weaker model's disagreements against a stronger market loses. The model may join later (Stage 4, conditional). |
| Data 1 | **Kept, and made central, pending validation** | A sharp book's de-vigged line (Pinnacle) is the industry's fair-price reference, but whether it beats Polymarket *in these markets* is tested first (Stage 1b), not assumed. |
| Data 2 | **Reframed** | "Do stats affect odds?" is trivially yes. The version that could make money is "does Polymarket react to injury news slower than Pinnacle?" (Stage 3, needs a timestamped status source). |

## What Makes This Cool

Chira already holds a **candidate** mispricing. arXiv 2609.12878 (588M Polymarket trades)
finds the favorite-longshot bias "surprisingly absent in Sports." Chira's headline 2 offers
a **hypothesis** for why: pooled NBA prices look well calibrated because thin markets are
underconfident (Cox slope +1.398) and liquid ones overconfident (+0.586), and the two cancel.
Whether the same cancellation explains that paper's broader sports pool is untested. And the
split is on **terminal** volume, which is not known at bet time, so the candidate is
unvalidated until Stage 1a rebuilds it on bet-time information. If it survives, the edge is
betting against the market's own liquidity-dependent miscalibration, not a better player model.

## Constraints

- **Holdout firewall.** Nothing model-derived touches 2025-26 until the holdout is opened.
  Purchased odds live in a separate `odds` schema that the pre-registered feature set never
  reads (see Stage 2).
- **Look-ahead, and a data gap.** Headline 2 stratifies on terminal cumulative volume. The
  census stores **only terminal volume** (`src/chira/schema.sql:61`, one `volume` column);
  there is no historical volume time series. So "volume at T-6h" cannot be computed from the
  snapshot, and Stage 1a must use a different bet-time proxy (below).
- **Execution cost is unmeasured.** The census holds a minute-level price series, not the
  order book. Historical spread is unknown; the fee schedule must be read on a stated date.
- **Capacity.** Thin markets are where the underconfidence lives and also where depth is
  smallest (NHL 2024-25 median volume $56k; NBA 2024-25 $296k). Stake size is capped by
  visible depth, not bankroll.
- **Venue access.** Polymarket and Pinnacle availability depends on each teammate's
  jurisdiction. Sportsbooks other than Pinnacle limit winning accounts.
- **No redistribution** (Polymarket ToS, E18). Purchased odds data is also internal-only, and
  the Chira repo is public (Pages is live). Its Actions artifacts can be downloaded by any
  signed-in GitHub user, so purchased data must never pass through them.
- **Timing.** The team is ~4 weeks ahead of PLAN.md's calendar (week 6 done 2026-09-25;
  week 7 is dated Oct 27). NBA 2026-27 tips late October. Stage 1a fits in that gap; 1b depends on the Stage 2 purchase.

### Stage 1 — two kill gates (1a: Lane E, ~2 weeks, target 2026-10-11, no purchases; 1b: after the historical pull, target NBA tip-off, conditional on the Stage 2 purchase)

**1a. Does the liquidity signal survive bet-time information?**

Pre-commit, in `notes/profit-stage1-spec.md`, committed before any number is computed:

- **One bet-time liquidity measure, one threshold.** Terminal volume is unavailable at bet
  time, and no historical volume series exists. The primary measure is the **count of price
  changes in the raw minute series from market open to T-6h**. It needs no new data, and
  price activity is a trading-activity proxy. **The low/high cut is a fixed threshold: the
  NBA 2024-25 median of this count, learned once and applied unchanged to 2025-26 and to live
  2026-27.** A current-season median would be look-ahead, since it isn't known until the
  season ends. If volume trends upward across seasons, this means more games fall "high" in
  later seasons. That is accepted and reported, not re-cut. The same fixed cut defines
  the **bet-time stratum** used everywhere else in this doc (1b, Signal P, the ledger).
  Stage 1a reports this measure's Spearman correlation with
  terminal volume first. If rho < 0.5, the proxy is declared unfit and 1a fails. No second
  proxy is tried on the same data.
- **Executable price at T-1h:** the last raw-series point at or before T-1h. The series is a
  normalized complementary price, not a quote, so it is treated as a mid.
- **Cost grid:** half-spread ∈ {1c, 2c, 3c} × fee ∈ {0, the Polymarket sports fee schedule as
  published on the spec's commit date}. The pass/kill cell is **2c half-spread, published fee.**
- **Recalibration map:** Cox intercept and slope per bet-time stratum, fit on NBA 2024-25 only.
  **For live 2026-27 trading, the map is refit once on 2024-25 + 2025-26 before tip-off,
  with the fixed cut unchanged, and then frozen for the season.** The refit is market-only,
  so it stays inside the firewall.
- **Bet rule:** take the side where the recalibrated probability exceeds the executable price
  plus half-spread plus fee; flat unit stake.
- **Evaluation:** NBA 2025-26 market-only. ROI with a date-clustered bootstrap 90% interval.

**1a kill criteria (any one kills the liquidity signal):**
- The proxy's rho with terminal volume < 0.5.
- The bet-time slope difference's 95% CI (date-clustered bootstrap) **computed on NBA 2025-26
  under the fixed 2024-25 cut** includes 0. It is also reported on 2024-25, but only the
  2025-26 result gates.
- The 2025-26 ROI 90% interval upper bound < 0 at the pass/kill cell. A positive point
  estimate with an interval straddling zero **passes 1a as a screen only**. The live
  paper gate is the real test.

**Honest caveat:** headline 2 already looked at both seasons, so 1a is not blind. Only live
2026-27 paper trading is blind.

**1b. Is Pinnacle actually fairer than Polymarket here?** (after the historical pull, Stage 2)

Compare de-vigged Pinnacle with Polymarket on realized outcomes, NBA 2024-25 and 2025-26
market-only, **by the bet-time stratum (the fixed price-change-count cut from 1a)**, so that
a live bet can be assigned to a 1b stratum when it is placed. Metrics: Brier, log loss, and a
paired game-level bootstrap of the difference. **If Pinnacle is not better than Polymarket in a
stratum** (the paired log-loss difference interval includes 0 or favors Polymarket), Pinnacle
is **not** used as the fair price in that stratum, and Signal P is not traded there.

- **At what time.** If the vendor has timestamped history, 1b runs at the decision time (T-1h)
  as its gate and at the close as a report. **If only closing prices can be bought**, 1b
  runs at the close only. Pinnacle's fairness at decision time is labeled untested, and Signal
  P's firing frequency is **not estimated historically**. It is learned from live paper
  bets. Timestamped history is therefore a strong vendor preference (Open Question 2).
- **Fallback books.** The consensus fallback can be used only if the purchase includes
  those books' history, so that it can pass 1b. If it doesn't, **Signal P does not exist
  without Pinnacle.**

### Signals (defined separately, logged separately, gated separately)

- **Signal L (liquidity recalibration):** exists only if 1a passes. Fires when the recalibrated
  probability beats Polymarket's executable price net of costs.
- **Signal P (Pinnacle deviation):** exists only in strata where 1b passes. Fires when
  de-vigged Pinnacle (live, at decision time) beats Polymarket's executable price net of costs.
- **Decision timing, one bet per game per signal.** Each signal is evaluated **once, at T-1h**
  (the backtested look), using the order-book snapshot taken at or within 60s before T-1h. If
  it fires, that is the game's only bet for that signal. There is no re-evaluation between
  T-1h and tipoff. Gate counts, rolling-CLV stops and bet-volume estimates all count
  **distinct games per signal**.
- **No combination rule.** When both fire on the same game, they are logged as two ledger
  rows with a shared `game_id`. If they point to **opposite sides**, neither is bet in any
  live-money phase, and the conflict is logged. After a 1a kill, only Signal P exists. If both
  1a and 1b fail, the profit track stops, and the result is written up as a finding.

### Stage 2 — data and ledger (Lanes D + E, October, before tip-off)

- **Storage.** Purchased odds and poller output go into a separate `odds` schema in a
  **gitignored** DuckDB file beside `data/census.duckdb`. The pre-registered feature set
  (frozen in PREREGISTRATION) never reads that schema. It is joined to the census by
  `game_id` **directly**, not through the week-7 feature store, which does not exist until
  Oct 27. After week 7 it may reuse the feature store's `ASOF JOIN` *helper* on its own
  tables. It never writes into the model's feature tables.
- **Historical pull:** one purchase of Pinnacle NBA (+NHL) moneylines for 2024-25 and 2025-26,
  with timestamps if available (Open Question 2). **De-vig: multiplicative (proportional)
  primary, Shin as the one reported sensitivity**, fixed in the Stage 2 spec before any CLV is
  computed.
- **Live Pinnacle source:** a vendor's live tier (Open Question 7). No scraping. Fallback if
  Pinnacle cannot be obtained: the de-vigged consensus of the sharpest books the chosen vendor
  carries. That fallback must itself pass 1b before it is used.
- **Live poller + order-book capture:** runs on an always-on machine the team controls (a
  teammate's machine or a small VPS). **Not** on GitHub Actions: Actions cron is best-effort
  and commonly 10+ minutes late (`.github/workflows/collector.yml:19`), too coarse for fills or
  news lag, and its artifacts are exposed on a public repo. Cadence: 1 minute from T-3h to
  tipoff. Vendor keys come from environment/secret storage, never the repo; logs print counts,
  never odds or keys. Output syncs to a private location, not the public repo.
- **Paper ledger (Lane E):** one row per (game, signal) firing: `game_id`, sport, signal
  (L/P), decision timestamp, side, the signal's **fair probability** (Signal L's recalibrated
  probability, or Signal P's de-vigged Pinnacle at decision), **bet-time liquidity count and
  stratum**, **pre-fill best price**, book-snapshot timestamp, paper stake, filled size, VWAP
  fill price, **fee charged**, **opposite-side conflict flag**, Pinnacle close, Polymarket
  close, result.
- **Paper fill rule:** fixed notional paper stake per bet (set in the ledger spec). The fill
  walks the order-book snapshot captured at or within 60s before the decision time, taking
  at most 20% of visible depth within 2c of best. Partial fills are recorded as filled. With no
  snapshot, there is no bet.

### Stage 3 — live paper trading (tip-off onward)

- Paper-trade every NBA Signal L/P firing on live 2026-27 games.
- **NHL rows are tagged `sport=NHL` and excluded from all gate counts and CLV aggregates.** The
  NHL market carries little information (Murphy resolution ~0.005-0.015). NHL can reach real
  money only through its own separate future gate, never through NBA's.
- **Data 2 news-lag study:** needs timestamped status changes. Candidate source: the NBA's
  official injury report, published on a fixed intraday schedule with a stated report time.
  This is the same "availability source" PLAN.md week 7 already says to prove or drop.
  **Capture window:** in addition to the 1-minute T-3h-to-tipoff trading window, the poller
  samples both venues at 1 minute for 30 minutes before and 60 minutes after **each scheduled
  report publication time** on game days. Measure minutes from the report time to a Polymarket
  move, against the same for Pinnacle. **Moves that come before the report** (news
  reached the market through other channels) are counted and reported as a share. They are
  excluded from the lag estimate, which is therefore a lag *relative to the official
  report*, not to first news. If the source is dropped, the study is deferred and reported as
  untested.

### Go-live gate (real money), per signal

A signal goes live only if **all** of the following hold:

- **≥ 200 live NBA paper bets on that signal.** Rough justification: a bet's P&L has a
  standard deviation near 1 unit at even-ish prices. Detecting a 2% edge from P&L alone needs
  on the order of (2.5 / 0.02)² ≈ 15,000 bets. CLV's per-bet standard deviation is a few cents,
  so detecting a ~1c mean needs on the order of (2.5 × 4 / 1)² ≈ 100-200. The 200 figure is
  **re-checked** once the backtest gives the real CLV standard deviation. If that implies
  more, the gate rises. It never falls below 200.
- **The right CLV metric for that signal, interval above zero.** Each is a game-level bootstrap
  90% interval after measured fill cost:
  - Signal L: CLV against de-vigged Pinnacle close (Pinnacle is independent of L's input).
  - Signal P: CLV against **Polymarket's own close** (did Polymarket move toward the bet?).
    CLV against Pinnacle would be positive by construction here.
- **Realized-outcome sanity check:** realized paper ROI ≥ 0 at the point estimate. In addition,
  the signal's fair probability on the bet side (from the ledger) is not rejected by a
  **Spiegelhalter Z test at alpha = 0.05** against outcomes.
- **Weighting:** CLV and ROI aggregates are **weighted by filled notional** (a partial fill
  counts for what was filled). Unweighted means are reported beside them. Only the weighted
  figures gate.
- The Assignment's legality confirmation is done for every teammate who will trade.

**Bet volume.** Stage 1a reports how often Signal L fires per game. 1b reports Signal P's rate
only if timestamped Pinnacle history was bought; otherwise P's rate is learned live. If a signal
cannot reach 200 by end of December, its window extends through the regular season (to
mid-April). **No go-live before 200 in any case.**

**Staking at go-live:** flat stakes, bounded by the paper fill rule's depth cap. Kelly sizing is
deferred to Stage 4.

**Live risk controls (once any real money is placed):**
- A team-agreed maximum bankroll, set before go-live. It is money the team can lose in full.
- Stop the signal if the rolling 100-bet CLV (its gate metric) turns negative, or if drawdown
  exceeds 30% of the bankroll. Restart only by passing the paper gate again.
- A weekly review of the ledger during live trading.
- If an account is limited, that venue drops out for that person. No workaround accounts.

### Stage 4 — conditional placeholder (not committed work)

### R3-1 — feasibility

**Problem**

> The deferral branch says Signal L's CLV is 'recomputed against de-vigged Pinnacle once [the Pinnacle history] arrives'. But the historical pull (Stage 2) covers only 2024-25 and 2025-26 moneylines, and no live Pinnacle feed runs in that branch until December. The 2026-27 games paper-traded from October to December would have no Pinnacle closes, so they cannot be recomputed and cannot count toward Signal L's Pinnacle-CLV go-live gate.

**Remedy**

> Put Pinnacle closes for the 2026-27 games from tip-off to the start of the live feed in the purchase scope, or state that October-December Signal L bets count only under the Polymarket-close CLV metric and that the 200-bet Pinnacle-CLV count starts when the live feed does.

### R3-2 — completeness

**Problem**

> Signal L's go-live metric is CLV against the de-vigged Pinnacle close. The document covers a temporary lack of Pinnacle (the deferral branch) and gives a consensus fallback for Signal P's live input. It does not define Signal L's gate metric if Pinnacle can never be obtained (vendor, cost or legality, Open Questions 1, 2 and 7). In that case Signal L has no defined path to go-live.

**Remedy**

> State Signal L's gate metric when Pinnacle is permanently unavailable. For example, CLV against Polymarket's own close, or against the 1b-validated consensus close. Otherwise state that Signal L cannot go live without Pinnacle.

### R3-3 — clarity

**Problem**

> The live firing price is undefined. The backtested bet rule compares the recalibrated probability with the series mid plus an assumed 2c half-spread plus fee. Live, the signals fire when the fair probability 'beats Polymarket's executable price net of costs', and a T-1h order-book snapshot exists. The document does not say whether the live decision uses the snapshot's best ask plus fee, or the mid plus the assumed half-spread. These can fire on different games, so the live policy may not match the backtest.

**Remedy**

> Define the live firing comparison for both signals, for example the snapshot best ask on the bet side plus the published fee. State how this relates to the backtested mid plus half-spread rule, and record the spread observed at decision in the ledger.

### R3-4 — feasibility

**Problem**

> The bet-time stratum and Signal L both depend on the count of price changes from market open to T-6h, with the cut learned on the historical raw minute series. The document does not say how this count is obtained live at decision time: which endpoint or collector, at what resolution, and whether it matches the post-hoc historical series. The poller starts only at T-3h, and the deferral branch captures only T-1h depth. A resolution or source mismatch would shift games across the fixed cut.

**Remedy**

> Specify the live source for the open-to-T-6h price-change count (for example the same prices-history endpoint and fidelity the census uses, fetched at T-6h or at decision time). Add a check that live counts match a post-hoc recomputation for the same games.

### R3-5 — clarity

**Problem**

> The news-lag study measures 'minutes from the report time to a Polymarket move', and separately counts moves that come before the report, but it never defines a move. There is no price-change threshold, no rule for linking a status change to a game's market, and no handling of reports with no status change for that game. Without these, the lag and the pre-report share cannot be computed reproducibly.

**Remedy**

> Define the move criterion (for example the first minute in which the price moves by at least X cents from its value at report time), the report-to-market matching rule, and which status changes qualify (for example players who changed status and are above a minutes threshold).

### R3-6 — clarity

**Problem**

**Remedy**

> State the data for the rho test. For example, NBA 2024-25 as the gate, with 2025-26 reported.
